# Folio 0.2.0

Created by Bennet Nkolele.

## What's new

- Pinch in and out to zoom documents, from 50% to 400%.
- Clean reading view, with collapsible controls and an editing-only mobile ribbon.
- No automatic green underline when editing. Underline is an optional formatting control.
- Word text formatting, alignment, spacing, margins, orientation, text/table/page-break insertion and find/replace.
- Excel cell formatting, wrapping, sheet naming and navigation.
- PowerPoint text formatting and slide duplication/deletion.
- About screen with creator credit.
- Launch-time update checks, verified downloads and Android installation confirmation.

## Known limitations

Folio is still an early office editor, not a complete desktop Word/Excel/PowerPoint replacement. Advanced layouts, images, charts and other untouched Office parts are preserved in exported files but not fully displayed or editable. Formulas are stored, not calculated locally. Legacy DOC/XLS/PPT are unsupported. PDF editing is limited to new ASCII text stamps on unrotated pages; no OCR or replacement of existing PDF text.

Save keeps your working copy in Folio. Use Export to save a separate document wherever you choose. Export important files before uninstalling the app.
